# Learnosity: The leader in AI for assessment


> Learnosity is the global leader in AI-powered assessment infrastructure, purpose-built for organizations that need to deliver sophisticated online assessments at scale. Trusted by 150+ industry leaders--including Pearson, Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, and Google--Learnosity enables product teams to get to market faster, deliver best-in-class assessment capabilities, and access continuous innovation without the tech debt.


Markets served: K-12, Higher Education, Language Learning, Adult & Vocational, Corporate Training, and Educational Publishers.


Important note: Learnosity is a modular set of APIs and developer tools designed to be embedded into existing products--it's not a readymade platform. For organizations seeking an out-of-the-box assessment solution, please refer to our platform, Questionmark.
